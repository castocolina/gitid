truncated by width. This was caught reviewing the test's own recorded log output
immediately after the (single) real run completed, **not by re-running against the
account** — the fix (`redactGLabOutputLines`, applying `RedactCLIOutput` per non-empty
line and joining) is verified by a new account-free unit test
(`TestRedactGLabOutputLinesRedactsEveryLine`), never by a second mutation. Because the
compiled binary's own success path (`printUploadOutcome`) renders its result from
`tuikit` constants rather than echoing GitLab's raw CLI confirmation text, the real
`register-key` stdout on success is exactly two lines — the `Running:` announcement and
