## Accomplishments

- The `internal/gitconfig/customkeys.go` engine (`SplitGitKey`, `ParseCustomKeysBlock`, `RenderCustomKeysBlock`, `EnsureCustomGitKey`) implementing D-F/D-G, with `CustomGitKeysBlockName` registered in `IsReservedBlockName` in the same commit (L4 destructive-loop prevention, proven non-vacuous below).
