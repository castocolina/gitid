<objective>
Give the upload section its second and third call sites and land the phase's one new
confirmation ceremony.

Three deliverables: (1) the Identity Manager's register-key modal — the manual
re-trigger surface for an identity that was created before `gh`/`glab` was
authenticated, or whose upload failed; (2) the rotate/repair key-ceremony's own
upload beat, reusing the component rather than reimplementing it; (3) D-04's
interactive old-key delete offer, the ONE place in this phase where autonomy is
deliberately absent because the action is remotely destructive.

Purpose: D-03 is explicit that the upload experience is a shared component reused by
rotate/clone/copy contexts, and D-04 is a USER OVERRIDE of the research
recommendation — the user chose an interactive delete offer over leave-and-report.
Both are load-bearing product decisions, not polish.

Output: upload reachable from the Identity Manager, present in both key-ceremony
modes, and the old-key cleanup offered safely.

**Two decisions resolved here with rationale:**

1. **09-RESEARCH.md Open Question 2 — when the delete offer resolves the old key's
   provider ID:** LAZILY, at result-screen time, from a fresh inventory read. An
   eager resolution during `KeyCeremonyPlan` would cache an ID captured before the
   user even confirmed the rotate, and a provider-side change in between would make
   gitid delete by a stale ID. Fresh-at-use is also what D-18's
   everything-transient posture and D-15's own dedupe-uses-fresh-inventory
   precedent already do everywhere else in this phase.
2. **09-RESEARCH.md Pitfall 3 — `actionMenuRows` and `actionMenuLabels()`:** the
   constant is REPLACED by a derivation from `len(actionMenuLabels())`. Adding the
   fifth row while a separate `= 4` constant still governs the layout math is a
   guaranteed desync; keeping two hand-maintained values and remembering to update
   both is the failure mode, not the fix. This is a scoped, reviewed correctness
   change, called out here so it is not mistaken for an accidental side effect.
</objective>
