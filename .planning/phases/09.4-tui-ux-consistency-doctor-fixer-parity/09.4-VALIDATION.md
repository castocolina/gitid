## Planner Notes on Coverage Gaps Closed Since This Draft

Three surfaces this draft did not enumerate were found during planning and now
have owners:

- **The `Makefile` `gate-visual-regression` `-run` regex** names the old screens
  literally. A renamed gate test does not fail under a stale pattern — it
  silently stops running. **Owner: 02-T2**, which requires reading the `-v` run
  lines rather than trusting the exit code.
- **`gate-copy-freeze` pins a string containing a removed tab's name**, so
  `design.go` and the `Makefile` must move together. **Owner: 02-T1.**
- **The sub-tab strip's height is stated in FOUR places**, three of them as
  literals — including one inside `renderStorage` that this phase's research
  did not name. **Owner: 04-T2**, which collapses them to one source BEFORE
  changing the height.

`<verify><human-check>`-class evidence in their plan's `<verification>`
section and harvested at end-of-phase (`workflow.human_verify_mode:
