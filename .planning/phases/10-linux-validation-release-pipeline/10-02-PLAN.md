## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Severity | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-10-02-01 | Tampering | `fedora:latest` base image (floating tag, not a pinned digest) | medium | accept | The job carries zero secrets and zero write-scoped tokens (no `permissions:` grant beyond inherited `contents: read`); a compromised image can at worst produce a false test result inside an isolated, ephemeral runner, caught by the separate `check`/`release` gates before any publish action ever runs. | T-10-02-02 | Elevation of Privilege | fedora job `permissions:` | low | accept | The job declares no `permissions:` key, inheriting the workflow's least-privilege top-level `contents: read` — verified by cmd/gitid/ci_fedora_test.go. |
